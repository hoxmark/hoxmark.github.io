Privacy Policy for Wovely

Last Updated: January 13, 2026

Overview

Wovely ("the App") is a knitting project management application developed for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Data Collection

What We Collect

Wovely does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

All data you enter into the app—including projects, patterns, yarn inventory, needle collections, photos, and counters—is stored locally on your device and optionally synced via Apple's iCloud if you have iCloud enabled.

Types of Data Stored Locally

  • Project names, descriptions, and notes
  • Pattern text and details
  • Yarn inventory information
  • Needle collection details
  • Counter values and history
  • Photos you attach to projects

Data Storage

Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your Wovely data may sync across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This sync is handled entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Third-Party Services

Wovely does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use:

  • Analytics tools
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media SDKs
  • Crash reporting services that collect personal data

Photos and Camera

Wovely requests access to your photo library and camera to allow you to:

  • Add photos to your knitting projects
  • Capture yarn colors using the camera

Photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers (unless you have iCloud Photo sync enabled through Apple).

Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • iOS device encryption
  • Apple's Keychain for sensitive data
  • iCloud encryption (if sync is enabled)

Children's Privacy

Wovely does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences interested in knitting and crafts.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information:

  • Access: View all your data within the app
  • Modify: Edit any information at any time
  • Delete: Remove individual items or delete the app to remove all data
  • Export: Your data can be exported (feature coming soon)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
