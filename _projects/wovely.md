## Wovely - Knitting Project Manager

A privacy-first iOS app for managing knitting projects, patterns, and yarn inventory. Built with SwiftData and CloudKit for seamless local storage and optional iCloud sync.

### Key Features
- **Complete Privacy**: No data collection, tracking, or analytics
- **Local Storage**: All data stays on your device using SwiftData
- **Optional iCloud Sync**: Sync across your Apple devices via CloudKit
- **Project Management**: Track knitting projects, patterns, and progress
- **Yarn Inventory**: Manage your yarn stash with photos and details
- **Pattern Storage**: Store and organize your knitting patterns
- **Counter Tools**: Built-in row and stitch counters

### Privacy by Design
Wovely is built with privacy as a core principle:
- ✅ No third-party analytics or tracking
- ✅ No advertising networks
- ✅ All data stored locally on your iPhone
- ✅ Optional Apple iCloud sync (handled by Apple)
- ✅ Full user control over all data

### Technical Details
- **Platform**: iOS (iPhone)
- **Framework**: SwiftUI + SwiftData
- **Sync**: Apple CloudKit (optional)
- **Storage**: Local device storage with iOS encryption
- **Photos**: Local storage only (unless iCloud Photos enabled)
